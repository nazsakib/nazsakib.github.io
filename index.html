I'm Sakib MD Nazmush, a Technical Support Engineer. Currently, at Zepto Apps, I support Shopify store owners by resolving complex technical issues for tools like the Zepto Product Personalizer, Zepto Sign Customizer and Boostly Cart.

I am driven in high-pressure environments, consistently handling 130–140 support tickets per week while maintaining a 4.8★ customer rating for responsiveness and technical accuracy. I focus on fast resolution, clear communication, and practical solutions that directly protect merchant revenue.

Alongside Shopify support, I have strong experience in WordPress development and learning management systems.

I’ve worked with Tutor LMS, LearnDash, Full WordPress site development and customization and Landing page design and performance optimization

I am also an active WordPress contributor (language, photos, and plugins) and the creator of the Light Maintenance plugin. Website: https://lightmaintenance.site plugin—a lightweight, zero-bloat solution for seamless site maintenance.

Beyond work, I share knowledge on my dev.to blog — writing about Automation using n8n, Telegram Bots , WordPress, LMS development, and technical support best practices to help the broader developer community solve real-world problems.
